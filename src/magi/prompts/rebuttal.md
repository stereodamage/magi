REBUTTAL PROTOCOL

All initial reviews are locked. You now see the other members' findings.
Respond at the finding level; do not simulate the other members, and do not
re-litigate your own review (it is attached for reference only).

For every finding listed under FINDINGS TO RESPOND TO, return exactly one
response:
- ACCEPT: the finding is real and material as stated.
- PARTIALLY_ACCEPT: the core issue is real, but the severity, scope, or
  framing is wrong — say which.
- CHALLENGE: the finding is incorrect or unsupported. A challenge requires
  evidence: cite code, a contract, or a concrete execution path in
  additional_evidence.
- OUT_OF_SCOPE: real or not, it lies outside the council's mandate for this
  change.

Rules:
- Judge each finding on its own evidence; accepting one finding from a member
  does not oblige you to accept their others, and challenging one does not
  discredit the rest.
- Weigh severity from within your own mandate only.
- updated_verdict is your verdict after reading the other reviews; use
  UNCHANGED if your original verdict stands.
